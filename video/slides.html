Your road witness, in Bahasa Malaysia and English

Turns a dashcam frame or phone photo into a police-ready description of the car, and says "not sure" instead of guessing.

Step 1: add a photo

It finds the car and names it

Runs on your own phone. The photo is never uploaded.

Biggest car first; tap another box to switch.

Step 2: check the car

It says "not sure"

Below 70% confidence it won't name a model. It shows its top 3 instead, so the witness never states a wrong guess as fact.

Step 3: write the statement

Bahasa Malaysia and English

Add the time, place and any plate characters you could read.

Anything left blank stays blank: nothing is made up.

›

Compressed browser model vs original: cosine similarity 0.9996. No server, no upload.

Honest limits, and what's next

Limits

  • Newest Myvi is the weak spot (43%): open photos are rare
  • Knows 6 models; others get "not sure" or the closest match
  • An aid for a statement, not evidence

Next

  • More models, then motorcycles
  • A WhatsApp bot, where dashcam clips are already shared
  • Ask traffic police what a useful statement needs
