How well does it work?

It knows six common cars: Perodua Axia, Bezza and Myvi; Proton Iriz, Persona and Saga. In tests on photographers it never trained on, its first guess was right 74% of the time and the right answer was in its top 3 95% of the time. It names a car only when it is at least 70% sure; at that level it was right 94% of the time.

Weak spots: the newest Myvi, older-looking Sagas, and cars that aren't one of the six, which it may confuse with the closest match. It is an aid for your statement, not evidence.
